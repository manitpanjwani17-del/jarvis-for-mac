# Code of conduct

Jarvis for Mac is for everyone. In issues, discussions and anywhere else connected to this project:

- **Be kind and patient.** People here range from first-time Mac users to experienced developers.
- **Be respectful.** No harassment, insults, discrimination or personal attacks, about anyone, for any reason.
- **Protect privacy.** Never post someone else's personal information, and never paste your own messages, contacts, numbers or logs with personal details.
- **Stay on topic** and assume good intent.

The maintainer may edit, hide or remove anything that breaks these rules, and may block people who repeatedly do. To report a problem privately, use GitHub's **Report content** option, or the private security report described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) for anything sensitive.
